Class: VGripper

VGripper()

This class represents the mesh gripper in the Three.js environment with its two contact sensors. Responsibility: It loads the URDF mesh of the Robotiq85 gripper. It adds also the contact sensor parallelepiped meshes to the fingers of the gripper-mesh and it also recovers informations from the Three.js scene like the tcp.
