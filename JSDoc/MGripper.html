Class: MGripper

MGripper(finger_contact_sensor_left_mesh, finger_contact_sensor_right_mesh)

This class represents one gripper with its contact sensors in the Ammo environment. Responsibility: it gathers informations necessary for managing and modelling the rigid bodies of contact sensors for a specific gripper in the Singleton Ammo physics world

Constructor

new MGripper(finger_contact_sensor_left_mesh, finger_contact_sensor_right_mesh)

Given the paralleliped meshes used for representing the contact sensors, create the respective twins in the Ammo environment (Only kinetic, not dynamic)
Parameters:

Methods

create_physical_finger_contact_sensor(finger_contact_sensor_mesh) → {btRigidBody}

Create the physical twin of the given Mesh in the Ammo environment. It will not be moved by the other bodies in the Ammo environment after a collision or by the gravity. It is moved kinematically by the movement of the robot in the Three.js environment and it is used for computing collisions with other bodies.
