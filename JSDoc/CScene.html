Class: CScene

CScene()

This class is the controller of the whole Scene and coordinates all the objectes between Three.js and Ammo environment. It uses the Singleton Pattern. Responsibility: It takes the inputs of the user; it setups/controls the scene and manages the animations of the robot and gripper.

Constructor

new CScene()

Setup the scene with the written informations in this constructor. Also initialize the various tools (tween,timer,callbacks,GUI).
