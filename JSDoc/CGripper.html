Class: CGripper

CGripper()

This class is the controller of the gripper. It assists the process of loading correctly the informations and syncronizing them between the View and Model Packages. Responsibility: syncronizes the data and position/orientation from the visual contact sensors (meshes) to the physical kinetic bodies in the Ammo environment.
